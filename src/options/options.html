Welcome to ClariWord AI

Highlight any word, phrase or sentence while you read and ClariWord explains what it means there — not just in the dictionary. It works right now in Demo mode with a built-in offline lexicon; connect your own backend below whenever you are ready.

AI backend

ClariWord never stores an AI provider key in the browser. The extension talks to a backend you control, and that backend holds the key. Until you configure one, Demo mode uses a built-in offline lexicon.
